About HELOC CONNECT

A premium home equity connection platform built around homeowner trust.

HELOC CONNECT was created to help homeowners better understand and access potential home equity lending options through trusted participating lender relationships.

Our Mission

Our mission is to help homeowners connect with trusted, secure, and competitive home equity lending options while making the process more transparent, efficient, and consumer-friendly. We believe homeowners deserve a clear resource that helps them understand potential borrowing power, compare possible financing pathways, and make more confident financial decisions.

Why HELOC CONNECT Exists

Many homeowners have built meaningful equity but are not fully aware of how that equity may support important financial goals. For qualified homeowners, a HELOC or second-lien financing solution may help with high-interest debt consolidation, home improvements, business funding, real estate investments, emergency liquidity, or major life expenses.

Consumer-First Platform

HELOC CONNECT does not charge consumers a direct platform fee to request lender matching options. Our compensation may be paid by participating lending partners, allowing homeowners to explore potential financing opportunities through our network without paying HELOC CONNECT directly.

Leadership

HELOC CONNECT is led by Daniel Emrani, CEO, with a focus on building a trustworthy platform that supports homeowners with clearer access to home equity financing information and participating lender pathways.

Important Lending Notice

HELOC CONNECT is not a lender and does not make credit decisions or guarantee approval, funding, rates, or loan terms. All financing decisions are made by participating lenders based on their underwriting guidelines.
